Vault directories at the root would mean the vault *is* the working directory. Scattering
`structure/` — and with it `permanent/` and `raw/` — through a user's repo is the failure the
containment rule exists to prevent, and the failure the body's pitfall 12 and checklist item
name in prose because prose is all they can do.
